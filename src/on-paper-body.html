What this page does and does not say

License text is self-reported by the host and displayed on the listing. Matching it to the register establishes whether a record exists and what state it is in — nothing more. It is never a determination that any listing is unlawful, and no individual host is named. There are lawful explanations for every category below, including licenses issued after the snapshot was taken and records that do not reconcile for clerical reasons. This page exists to size a population an agency could audit, not to accuse anyone. New to the rules? Start with what the law actually says.

The calendar test

This one still rests on displayed text. The DLCP register records that a short-term rental license exists; it does not record whether it is the hosted or the unhosted kind. That half of the test comes from the listing, so it did not get stronger when the records were matched. Treat it as the weaker claim on the page.
