Spread the word

A print-ready flyer kit for the In Plain Sight tools. Every code below points at a live, free tool — no account, no app, nothing leaves the reader's phone.

How to use it

  1. Print it. Use your browser's Print (⌘P / Ctrl-P). Each flyer prints on its own page — plain black-and-white is fine and scans just as well.
  2. Post it. Community boards work best: libraries, laundromats, rec centers, coffee shops, tenant clinics, mutual-aid pantries, your building lobby, an ANC meeting.
  3. Tear-off tabs. The strip along the bottom is pre-cut lines — snip up between them so people can take a tab with the address.
  4. Test the code with your own phone camera before you post, so you know it lands where you expect.

What's inside

  • A whole-family poster (links to the hub).
  • One flyer each: deposit/move-in, renter rights, scam check, lease check, DC records, and trash/recycling.

A note on honesty

These say plainly: a free civic tool, not a government site, not legal advice. Keep that line — it's what makes them trustworthy on a wall next to everything else.
